## Outcome and scope

- Add activity breakdown with workout type/duration/source, step count and freshness.

- Show workouts as subsets in Apple-active mode; use other active energy when walking energy cannot be isolated.

- Explain selected source priority, uncertainty and why displayed subsets do not sum as extra calories.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Walking/strength detail never changes the canonical energy total.

- [ ] No precise walking-calorie estimate is inferred from steps when unsupported.

- [ ] Unknown workout energy and read-access ambiguity remain visible.

## Validation and evidence

- Display checks using overlapping sources and missing workout energy; reconcile with R013 totals.
