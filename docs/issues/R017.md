## Outcome and scope

- Document R2 vs Supabase choice and verify current provider limits; implement storage meter, configurable compression/retention and upload caps.

- Export JSON/CSV/media manifest plus checksum-verified archive and document encrypted local database/media backup and restore.

- Add archive-before-delete and confirmed retention actions; preserve camera-roll originals.

- Document provider alerts/cap behavior, pause recovery and API/email/AI spending controls without promising free unlimited use.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Usage and retention settings are visible; oversize/over-quota uploads fail safely.

- [ ] Restore drill reconstructs ledger/revisions/provenance and media references from backup.

- [ ] Deletion affects only app copies and explicitly explains any remaining external Health records.

## Validation and evidence
