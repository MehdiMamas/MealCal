## Outcome and scope

- Store meal/photo drafts and operation IDs securely on device without a connection.

- Replay uploads and mutations with durable idempotency, revision conflict review and user-visible pending state.

- Recover interrupted queues after restart and preserve deletes/edits without recreating superseded entries.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Airplane-mode capture survives app restart without losing photos or entered quantities.

- [ ] Reconnect/retry produces one meal/media record and one desired Health result.

- [ ] Concurrent web edits trigger conflict resolution rather than silent overwrites.

## Validation and evidence

- Airplane mode, process kill, partial upload, expired session and cross-device conflict tests on iPhone.

## Boundaries

Implement only this issue; do not start later phases or optional enhancements.
