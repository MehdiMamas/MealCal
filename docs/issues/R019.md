## Outcome and scope

- Implement all/half/25%/custom/unknown and consumed grams per food; distinguish served quantity from eaten quantity.

- Support mixed plates, sauces, repeated helpings and linked batches with next-day leftovers as new consumption events.

- Preview fractional estimates for approval and create revisions/Health desired changes from approved consumed quantities.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 600 kcal served and approved 50% consumed yields 300 kcal; fully eaten/untouched items scale independently.

- [ ] Unknown consumption remains uncertain rather than assumed full consumption.

- [ ] Leftovers eaten tomorrow add only the newly consumed amount on tomorrow's day.

## Validation and evidence

- Per-item mixed-plate and multi-serving math, edit/retry and next-day batch tests. Covers T-08.

## Boundaries

No automatic photo-only portion commitment; minimal P0 consumed amount entry remains available in R002.
