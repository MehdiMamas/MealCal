## Outcome and scope

- Default activity contribution to off; support apple_active and workouts_only with explicit walking/strength/other switches and separate net toggle.

- Use one Health active-energy aggregate or selected deduplicated workouts; never add workout energy to overlapping active total.

- Store consented source/freshness only; show unavailable expenditure as unknown and legacy report totals as historical evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Off mode leaves meal totals unchanged and excludes activity from net.

- [ ] Apple active mode subtracts active energy once; steps do not generate extra calories.

- [ ] Workouts-only deduplicates overlapping source records or exposes uncertainty; net is labeled simplified, not full energy balance.

## Validation and evidence

- Known overlapping walking/strength/active fixtures, duplicate workouts, unavailable data and mode switching. Covers T-11/T-12.

## Boundaries

Implement only this issue; do not start later phases or optional enhancements.
