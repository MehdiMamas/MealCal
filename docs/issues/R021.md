## Outcome and scope

- Define occurred_at_utc, original IANA timezone, original local date and home_timezone/event_local aggregation rules.

- Handle imports with missing time/timezone using an explicit user-approved interpretation and precision metadata.

- Provide shared date-boundary calculations and define reaggregation for edits moving entries across days.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 23-hour and 25-hour days use timezone boundaries, never fixed 24-hour arithmetic.

- [ ] Travel and near-midnight meals keep their intended dates under the chosen policy.

- [ ] Historical corrections invalidate every affected old/new day.

## Validation and evidence

- DST spring/fall transitions, repeated local times, timezone travel, month/year boundaries and missing imported timezone fixtures. Covers T-14.
